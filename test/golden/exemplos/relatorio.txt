# Thermal drift of the reference cavity

Across 12 independent runs on the Zygo interferometer, the estimated effect was 4.237 ± 0.008 mm, against a baseline of 1.102 ± 0.015 mm, at 21.4 ± 0.3 °C.

Theorem 1. The reported effect is 4.237 ± 0.008 mm, whose relative uncertainty is 0.2%. Its central value is 4.237, stated to the precision the uncertainty supports and not one digit beyond it.

Theorem 1.1. The effect is resolved to better than one percent of its own value. The digits shown are the digits the measurement earned.

Theorem 2. The baseline of 1.102 ± 0.015 mm was measured under the same conditions and subtracted from every run before the effect was estimated.

Theorem 3. This report carries one caveat, and it is the following: the cavity was not re-evacuated between the seventh and eighth runs.

Theorem 4. The data supporting this report are deposited at doi:10.5281/zenodo.9999999 and released under the CC BY 4.0 license. No result reported above depends on material that is not in that deposit.

Theorem 5. The analysis plan was registered before collection began, and no outcome was added to it afterwards.

Prepared by R. Tavares on 2026-09-05, reviewed by H. Duarte. By Theorem 1, the value above is reported without further correction.
